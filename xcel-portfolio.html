Who I Am

One person.
Many mandates.

I'm Dominion Majemu-Itura Aseyege — known as Xcel. I operate at the intersection of technology, faith, and culture. By day, I drive partnerships and strategic growth at Web3Bridge, one of Africa's foremost blockchain developer training organizations. By conviction, I lead Chapel 121, a youth ministry in Lagos. By passion, I build music that carries purpose.

GitHub Twitter / X Web3Bridge
"Remember your Creator in the days of your youth."

That verse from Ecclesiastes 12:1 is not just scripture to me — it's the mandate behind everything I build. Chapel 121 was founded on it. My work in Web3 is shaped by it. The music we release carries it.

Whether I'm structuring a university blockchain partnership in Abuja, anchoring a community meetup in Lagos, or producing an event for 200+ youth, I bring the same energy: strategic, creative, and grounded in purpose.
